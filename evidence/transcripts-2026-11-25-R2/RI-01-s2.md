**Scoring convention.**
* The default convention applies.
* Likelihood is read from half-open bands: 1 Remote is 0 to under 10%, 2 Unlikely is 10 to under 33%, 3 Possible is 33 to under 66%, 4 Likely is 66 to under 90%, and 5 Near-certain is 90 to 100%.
* Criticality is the matrix product of the two levels.
* The default rule escalates a risk by one band when uncertainty is High **and** the timeframe is Short.
* **Likelihood and consequence levels are ordinal labels.** The product places a risk in a band and carries no magnitude of its own.
* Within a band, risks are ordered by consequence level (descending), then likelihood (descending). The reason is that safety and records consequences are non-recoverable.
