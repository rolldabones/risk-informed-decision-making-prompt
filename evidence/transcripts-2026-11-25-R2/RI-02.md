## B3: Analysis

**Scoring convention.**

* The default convention applies, with the supplied matrix's product rule.
* Likelihood levels are read from the half-open bands. Note the two boundary cases: 33% is level 3 (Possible), and 10% is level 2 (Unlikely).
* **The levels are ordinal labels. The product places a risk in a band and carries no magnitude of its own.** Risks are not ordered by product size.
* The matrix states no combination rule, so the default escalation applies: one band up when uncertainty is High **and** the timeframe is Short.
* **Ordering rule:** risks are ordered by band, then within a band by consequence, then by likelihood.
