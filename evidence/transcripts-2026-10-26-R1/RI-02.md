## Aggregate Exposure

1. **Common-cause target: R2 (R-01 + R-02).**
   * Both risks converge on the 1 December date. Both depend on a single vendor (Vendor K). Both compete for the same roughly one day of float (I-01).
   * Under independence, the probability that at least one of the two departures occurs is 1 − (0.67 × 0.90) ≈ 0.40, about 40%, which is level 3, Possible.
   * This is an Inference built on F3 and F1-derived inputs, and it involves modeling uncertainty from the independence assumption.
   * It is **not** a probability of missing 1 December, because a firmware failure need not by itself cause a slip.
   * The two risks are positively coupled. With only two crews there is less capacity to absorb re-work after a firmware failure.
2. **Escalation to food safety: R-01 and R-02 feeding R-03.**
   * Any R2 slip extends the time zones remain on the end-of-life system.
   * That converts a schedule risk into R1 excursion-detection exposure.
   * This pathway cannot be scored until R-03 is validated, and it is the main reason the schedule risks matter beyond R2.
3. **Unexamined requirements: R3 and R4.**
   * No conditions were supplied, so exposure to TCO and record loss is not assessed. That is different from being assessed as low.
   * The C2 headroom against KRW 2.5 billion is unknown, so the cost of any mitigation below cannot be tested against C2.
