### Scoring convention

* **Convention used:** the default convention, applied unchanged.
  * Likelihood uses half-open bands: 1 is 0 to under 10%, 2 is 10 to under 33%, 3 is 33 to under 66%, 4 is 66 to under 90%, and 5 is 90 to 100%.
  * Consequence is the worst credible outcome on a 1–5 scale against the most affected requirement.
* **Boundary readings:**
  * 10% reads as level 2 (Unlikely), because 10% is that band's included lower bound.
  * 33% reads as level 3 (Possible), because 33% is that band's included lower bound.
  * Both point estimates sit exactly on a boundary, so the credible ranges are reported as ranges of levels.
* **Criticality:** no matrix was supplied, so no band is assigned, no arithmetic applies and no escalation is made.
* **Ranking without a band:** risks are ranked on the likelihood and consequence pair. The rule is consequence level first, then likelihood level. I chose consequence-first because R1 carries the food-safety exposure that set the stakes band. The levels are ordinal labels, and the ranking claims no magnitude.
